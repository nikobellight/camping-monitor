⚠️ Important — Please read before proceeding

🔍

Monitoring service only. CampSiteAlert checks availability on your behalf. We do not book campsites for you. When we alert you, you must complete the booking yourself directly on the park booking website.

❌

No availability guarantee. CampSiteAlert monitors availability only — not a reservation service. If no campsite opens during your monitoring period, your alert expires automatically. No spot found = no refund.

⏰

Checks are periodic, not instant. We check every 5–15 minutes depending on how close your arrival date is. A spot may open and be booked by someone else before your alert is sent.
